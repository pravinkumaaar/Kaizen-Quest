- **Altered intercellular communication** = the phone lines between city departments go haywire (chronic inflammation — "inflammaging")

**The key insight:** These aren't inevitable. They're *mechanisms*. And mechanisms can be targeted. That's what makes this a revolution, not just a wish.

## The Counterintuitive Truth

**Everyone thinks longevity = extending life. The real goal is extending *healthspan* — the years you live without disease, disability, or cognitive decline.**

Here's what the hype gets wrong:

1. **"Take this supplement and live to 150"** — The supplement industry (NAD+ precursors like NMN, resveratrol, etc.) is running *years* ahead of the evidence. NMN was just removed from the FDA's supplement list in late 2022 because it's being investigated as a *drug*. Most longevity supplements have shockingly weak human data. The mouse results are exciting; mice are not humans.

2. **"Aging is natural and can't be changed"** — Nature doesn't care about you after reproductive age. There's no evolutionary pressure to keep you alive at 80. But that doesn't mean biology is fixed. We already changed it: vaccines, antibiotics, and sanitation added ~40 years to average lifespan in a century. The next wave targets the *rate* of aging itself.

3. **"It's all genetics"** — Twin studies show genetics explains only ~20-30% of lifespan variation. The rest is environment, behavior, and increasingly, *intervention*. The most powerful longevity "drug" right now is **exercise** — specifically zone 2 cardio and resistance training. It upregulates virtually every positive hallmark. It's free. Almost nobody does enough of it.

4. **The real gap:** The public thinks longevity is about *adding years to life*. The cutting edge is about **reversing biological age** — and early data from epigenetic reprogramming (Yamanaka factors, partial reprogramming) suggests this may actually be possible. Altos Labs is betting $3B on this. The question isn't *if* but *when* and *for whom*.
