## 📚 Energy & Climate: The Next Mega-Trend

**Why This Specific Aspect Matters:**
The global energy transition is no longer a distant policy aspiration — it's an industrial revolution happening in real time, driven by economics rather than altruism. Solar and battery costs have fallen 90% in 15 years, making renewables the cheapest new electricity source in most of the world, which means the question is no longer "if" but "how fast" and "who profits." For investors, this is a multi-decade structural shift comparable to the rise of the internet, with winners and losers being determined right now.

**The Core Concept (5-Minute Explainer):**
Think of the energy transition like the shift from horse-drawn carriages to automobiles — except this time, the "cars" (solar, wind, batteries, EVs) are already cheaper than the "horses" (coal, oil, gas) for most uses. The core concept is **cost parity**: when a new technology becomes cheaper than the incumbent, adoption doesn't happen gradually — it explodes. We're at that inflection point now. In 2025, over 60% of new electricity capacity added globally was renewable. The International Energy Agency, historically conservative, now projects renewables will surpass coal as the world's largest electricity source by 2028. But here's what makes this truly massive: electricity is only ~20% of final energy use. The next frontier is electrifying transportation, heating, and industrial processes — which means the addressable market for clean energy is 5x what we've built so far.

**The Counterintuitive Truth:**
