**Layer 2 — Storage & Grid:** This is where the real bottleneck lives. **Layer 3 — Demand Electrification:** EVs, heat pumps, data centers, and industrial electrification are creating *more* electricity demand, not less. The key mental model: **We're not swapping one energy source for another. We're building a second energy system on top of the first while the first one is still running.** That's why investment needs are so staggering—and why the opportunity is so large.

**The Counterintuitive Truth:**

Everyone thinks the energy transition is about *replacing* oil and gas. It's not. The IEA projects fossil fuel demand peaks this decade but doesn't collapse—it plateaus. The real story is that **total energy demand is surging**, and renewables are racing to meet *new* demand while slowly eating into the old. This means fossil fuel companies aren't going extinct anytime soon, and the transition plays are more nuanced than "long solar, short oil."

The second counterintuitive truth: **The bottleneck isn't technology—it's permitting, supply chains, and skilled labor.** We know how to build solar farms and batteries. What we can't do is get permits fast enough, train enough electricians, or mine enough lithium and copper quickly enough. The companies that solve *logistics and bottlenecks* may make more money than the companies making the headline products.
