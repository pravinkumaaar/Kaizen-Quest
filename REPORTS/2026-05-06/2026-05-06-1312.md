### The Counterintuitive Truth

**What everybody gets wrong:** They think the AI energy story is just about building more solar panels and wind farms.

**Reality:** Intermittent renewables don't solve the AI problem alone. Here's why:

The math is brutal. An AI data center needs **reliable, 24/7, always-on power** — call it a **baseload requirement of 99.9% uptime**. Solar? Intermittent (20-30% capacity factor). Wind? 30-45%. To guarantee 500MW at 99.9% reliability from solar alone, you'd need to overbuild by 4-5x AND add massive battery storage—driving the effective cost per delivered MWh through the roof.

That's why tech giants are literally **buying existing nuclear plants**, investing in **SMRs** (which, to be fair, aren't commercially proven at scale yet), and signing long-term PPAs with natural gas producers as a bridge.
