### The Core Concept (5‑Minute Explainer)  
**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** is the field that gives computers the ability to **learn from data, make decisions, and improve autonomously** without being explicitly programmed for each task. At its heart lies **machine learning**, where algorithms detect patterns in massive datasets and adjust their internal parameters to predict outcomes. Think of a child learning to recognize a cat: the child sees many pictures, extracts features (pointy ears, whiskers), and gradually forms a mental model. An AI system does the same mathematically, but at a scale and speed impossible for a human. This **self‑optimizing loop**—data → model → prediction → feedback → refinement—lets AI tackle everything from image recognition to language translation, from routing traffic to designing new materials.  

### The Counterintuitive Truth  
Most people assume AI will **replace humans en masse** and that its impact will be a sudden, binary switch. The reality is far more subtle: **AI is an augmenting force, not a wholesale substitute**.
