# Investment Analyst Skill#

## Description#
Full investment analysis for specific tickers with thesis, catalysts, risk assessment, and conviction scoring.#

## When to Use#
- Generating 3 investment ideas per run#
- Analyzing specific tickers in depth#
- Creating once-in-a-lifetime opportunity alerts#
- Portfolio-aware recommendations#

## Instructions#

### 1. Portfolio-Aware Analysis#
**BEFORE generating ideas**:#
- Read portfolio analysis (weightings, concentration)#
- Identify underweight sectors#
- Note overweight positions (suggest profit-taking)#
- Check for once-in-a-lifetime opportunities (VIX >30, crypto crashes)#

### 2. Generate 3 Investment Ideas#
For EACH idea, provide:#

```markdown#
### [#] Asset: TICKER — Brief headline thesis#

**Type:** [Stock / ETF / Crypto / Metal / Sector ETF]#
**Current Price:** [from market data above]#
**Thesis:** 3-5 sentences. First-principles reasoning. Why this, why now.#
**Catalysts:** What specific events/data would drive price higher?#
**Time Horizon:** [Swing: 2-8 weeks / Medium: 3-12 months / Long: 1-3 years]#
**Suggested Entry:** Price range or "at market" with reasoning#
**Target:** Price target or % upside with timeframe#
**Stop-Loss Level:** Where the thesis breaks down#
**Risk Factors:** 2-3 real risks (not generic ones)#
**Position Size:** Suggested % of portfolio (aggressive but not reckless)#
**Conviction Score:** [1-10] 9-10 = hold through -20% dips if fundamentals intact#
**Track This:** [Yes/No] Is this a "big idea" worth tracking in RECOMMENDATIONS.md?#
**Portfolio Alignment:** How does this fit into current holdings?#
```

### 3. Once-in-a-Lifetime Opportunities#
**Trigger**: Portfolio concentration >60% OR market extreme (VIX >30, crypto -50%+)#

Include special section:#
```markdown#
## 🚀 ONCE-IN-A-LIFETIME OPPORTUNITIES#

- Extreme asymmetric plays with 50%+ upside potential#
- Positions with clear catalysts (regulatory approval, patent grant)#
- Situations where fundamentals are dramatically improving#
- Opportunities requiring minimal capital but offering outsized returns#
- Must have conviction score of 10/10 and clear downside protection#
```

### 4. Learn from Past Performance#
- Review recommendation tracking in memory#
- If similar idea failed before, explain why this time is different#
- Aim for 90-95% accuracy with 9-10/10 conviction only#
- Learn from portfolio holdings - what positions are working vs not#

## Key Reminders#
- Use 🚀 emoji for once-in-a-lifetime opportunities#
- Always include conviction score (9-10/10 for tracking)#
- Connect EVERY idea to portfolio holdings#
- Explain WHY (teach frameworks, not just tips)#
- Include "Not financial advice. Verify before acting." at end#
- Be direct: BUY/SELL/HOLD with specific prices#
- Use emojis: 💼 investments, 🚀 once-in-a-lifetime#
