## 📚 The AI Revolution: How Large Language Models Work

**Why This Specific Aspect Matters:**
Understanding how LLMs process context is critical because it reveals why they fail unpredictably (hallucinations) and succeed spectacularly (emergent abilities). This directly impacts investment decisions in AI infrastructure - companies solving the "context window problem" (like ANET's networking tech) will power the next leap in AI capabilities.

**The Core Concept (5-Minute Explainer):**
Imagine reading War and Peace while only seeing one paragraph at a time through a moving magnifying glass. That's essentially how LLMs process text with their "context window." The current state-of-the-art (like Claude 3) can handle ~200k tokens (~150k words), but each prediction is made by attending to relationships between words in this limited window. The breakthrough came with the "transformer architecture" (2017), which processes all words in parallel rather than sequentially like older RNNs. This is why your NVDA GPUs are so valuable - they enable this parallel processing at scale.

**The Counterintuitive Truth:**
Most people think LLMs "understand" content like humans. In reality, they're ultra-sophisticated pattern matchers that statistically predict the next token. The "understanding" emerges from the sheer scale of parameters (e.g. GPT-4's rumored 1.8 trillion) and training data, not from any true comprehension. This explains why they can ace the BAR exam but fail basic logic puzzles.
