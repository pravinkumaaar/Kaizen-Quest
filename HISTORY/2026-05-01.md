Microsoft alone is planning a $80B data center buildout. That's not a tech problem — it's an infrastructure and energy bottleneck.

Now, here's where it gets interesting for your portfolio:

**Small Modular Reactors (SMRs)** are the category to watch. Instead of building massive, decade-long, multi-billion-dollar nuclear plants, companies like **Oklo (OKLO)** and **NuScale** are designing compact, factory-built reactors that can be deployed near data centers. Think of it like the shift from mainframe computers to servers — same power, radically different deployment model.

Everyone thinks of nuclear as the "old, dangerous, politically toxic" energy source. Three Mile Island. Chernobyl. Fukushima. The popular narrative is that nuclear is dying.

4. **We're not building enough renewables fast enough** to handle the AI power surge + EV adoption + electrification of everything. Nuclear fills the 24/7 baseload gap that wind and solar cannot.

The gap between perception (nuclear is dying) and reality (nuclear is resurgent, driven by AI demand and bipartisan support) is where the *opportunity* lives. Markets are still pricing in 2015 risk assumptions on nuclear companies. The fundamentals shifted years ago.
