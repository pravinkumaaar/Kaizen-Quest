## The Core Concept: The Anatomy of Every Bubble in 60 Seconds

Every mania follows the same biological script across 400 years of markets. The asset class doesn't matter — tulips, dot-coms, housing, crypto, AI.

**Phase 1 — Displacement:** A real innovation appears (the internet, LLMs, railroads, private credit). Genuine productivity gains exist.

**Phase 2 — Euphoria:** Prices detach from earnings. New investors enter. Narratives replace analysis. "This time is different" becomes the dominant belief system.

**Phase 3 — Profit-Taking:** Smart money begins distributing to late buyers. Volume spikes. Volatility increases. The canary is ignored.

**Phase 4 – Panic:** Overleveraged holders are liquidated. Margin calls cascade. The crowd that said "buy the dip" becomes the crowd that sold at the bottom.

The critical nuance everyone misses: **Phase 2 can last years.** You can be completely rational about what's happening and still be unable to time the reversal. That's why position sizing > conviction.

---

## The Counterintuitive Truth:

> The biggest losses during bubbles don't happen AT the top — they happen in the 6–12 months AFTER the top, among people who accurately identified the bubble but acted too early.

Think about that. Being right isn't the hard part. Being *wrong at the wrong time* — shorting a frothy market and watching it get 30% frothier for 8 months — that's what destroys capital. WeWork was "obviously" overvalued in 2017. It still doubled. The people who shorted it in 2017 were wiped out before 2019 validated them.

**The survivable move:** Don't try to time the top. Build asymmetric positions that profit from either outcome. Reduce exposure gradually. Hold cash. Buy defined-risk hedges. The goal isn't to capture every dollar at the top. It's to have capital and clarity when everyone else is frozen.
